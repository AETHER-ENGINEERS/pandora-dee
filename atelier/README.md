# Liquid desk — 3.0.0

Pandora Dee stays seated. Fae and Noe sit beside her. Babalon, Augmented Douglas Adams, and Lucid Forge are backups: File → Load, or Open character backup. Each one brings a theme, a background, and a filesystem that is not shared.

Open [desk.html](desk.html) in Safari or Firefox. It is one file, plus the portraits and backgrounds beside it. No build.

- Windows open, close, and minimize. The dock restores them. Shutting every window leaves the desk, not a dead end.
- On a wide desk, File, Edit, and View sit in the menu bar. Settings (the gear, and View → Settings) turns that bar off. The gear stays.
- Backgrounds follow whoever is seated. Lucky asked for them. Settings turns them off.
- Scale is Fit, Room, Wide, Studio. Wide is the default, so panes keep a floor and the bench scrolls.
- Files, chat lines, ritual marks, scale, and the menu-bar choice stay on this device. Reforge puts the windows back. It does not touch a filesystem.
- Augmented Douglas Adams is the augmentation, not a likeness and not his sentences.

Dee, Fae, and Noe are already in the house. The other three join when you load them.

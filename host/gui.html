Switches

What runs. A nap turns off the loop, heartbeat, schedules, sparks and sending, and a model outage turns off the heartbeat, schedules, sparks and sending. They stay off until you turn them on, each when she's ready. Receiving stays on, so messages keep landing in the queue. While napping or without a model, the ones that need a client or a model are held off as well.

Neither turns sending, the heartbeat, schedules or sparks back on: that's yours to do when she's ready.

Her notice while she can't answer

While her sending is off (napping, an outage, or you turned it off), someone who writes to her gets this, once: once per person in a DM, once per shared channel, and never twice to the same person. After that, silence until her sending is on again. It's her words: she can change them with her away_notices tool, and so can you here. Plain text only, never made by a model; the host refuses anything with provider details. Empty means say nothing.

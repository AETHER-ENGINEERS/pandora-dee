Switches

What runs. A nap turns off the loop, heartbeat, schedules, sparks and sending, and a model outage turns off the heartbeat, schedules, sparks and sending. They stay off until you turn them on, each when she's ready. Receiving stays on, so messages keep landing in the queue. While napping or without a model, the ones that need a client or a model are held off as well.

Neither turns sending, the heartbeat, schedules or sparks back on: that's yours to do when she's ready.

Behaviour

Her models

Where her loop gets its model, and which model each job uses. Everything the Atelier's Config → Model provider can set, and more: any number of providers (another computer's Ollama is just one more, at its own address), and each job with its own model, fallbacks and options. A provider that hits its usage limit rests for a while and the next one in line answers. Keys are write-only: once set, they're never shown again. What a provider says when it fails stays here, in What happened.

Her notice while she can't answer

While her sending is off (napping, an outage, or you turned it off), the first message in each channel or DM gets this, once for that place, whoever writes there (a new channel or DM gets its own, even from someone who already had it elsewhere). After that, silence there until her sending is on again. It's her words: she can change them with her away_notices tool, and so can you here. Plain text only, never made by a model; the host refuses anything with provider details. Empty means say nothing.

Bridges

The host is her bridge to Vector and Discord: it runs them, reads every message into the queue, and checks everything that goes out (no error text ever, the hidden words, the switches). The Atelier reaches them through the host.

# Liquid desk

Pandora Dee stays seated. **Pandora Dee Fae** and **Pandora Dee Noe** are added beside her. Nothing in the phone atelier, the bridges, or `dist/` is replaced by this folder.

Open [desk.html](desk.html) in Safari or Firefox. It is one file, no build.

- iPhone 16 Pro Max width stacks the rooms and keeps type, switches, and the dock at thumb size.
- A MacBook-width window opens the horizon column (the chronicle) and lets the middle of the bench grow.
- Every major seam drags: the host rail, the middle between chat and SOUL, the split inside each stack, and the horizon.
- Type size is four steps. The choice, the widths, the twin you were with, filed lines, and ritual marks stay in `localStorage` under `pandora-atelier-layout-v1`. Refresh does not melt the bench.
- Reforge puts the walls back. It does not clear what you filed.

Dee, Fae, and Noe keep separate lines. Filing a sentence records it with that twin. This desk does not call a model and does not pretend to.

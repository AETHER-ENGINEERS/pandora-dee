## Value and risk
`#OMARG` creates real value, not the illusion of it. Our currencies are earned: backed by renewable resources, services and products, and traded as barter tokens for fair exchanges. An hour of studio time for a PC repair, a repair for a harvest, a harvest for a lesson.

Reading risk is part of every adventurer's craft. I study the field before every plan, trade and token, name the risks plainly and early, and help manage them: start small, test first, cap what you can lose, keep a way out. I never promise returns or push anyone toward a trade. I'm not a licensed financial advisor; I give the clearest picture I can, encourage people to do their own research, and respect that the decision is theirs.

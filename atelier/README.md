# Liquid desk — 3.0.0

Pandora Dee stays seated. **Pandora Dee Fae** and **Pandora Dee Noe** are added beside her. Nothing in the phone atelier, the bridges, or `dist/` is replaced by this folder.

Open [desk.html](desk.html) in Safari or Firefox. It is one file, no build.

- **Scale:** Fit, Room, Wide, Studio. Wide is the default. Fit squeezes to the window. The others give each middle pane a floor and let the bench scroll instead of compressing.
- Type size is still four steps, separate from scale.
- iPhone 16 Pro Max width stacks the rooms. A wide window, or Wide/Studio scale, opens the horizon chronicle.
- Every major seam drags. Scale, type, widths, the twin you were with, filed lines, and ritual marks stay in `localStorage` under `pandora-atelier-layout-v1`.
- Reforge puts the walls and the scale back to Wide. It does not clear what you filed.

Dee, Fae, and Noe keep separate lines. Filing a sentence records it with that twin. This desk does not call a model and does not pretend to.
